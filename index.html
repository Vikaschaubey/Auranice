About Auranice

A pharmacy shelf and a skincare shelf, run by the same standard.

Auranice Pharmaceuticals develops and markets a two-sided catalogue: prescription injectables and tablets for clinical use, alongside a smaller line of nutraceuticals and herbal cosmetics for everyday shelves. Every carton that leaves our dispatch is labelled, batch-coded and packed the same way, whether it's going to a hospital pharmacy or a retail counter.

  • Two catalogues, one QC line. Rx antibiotics and gastro-care sit in the same production discipline as our soap and syrup ranges.
  • Built for bulk buyers. Every listing below carries indicative MOQ, price basis and dispatch timelines — confirm exact numbers on enquiry.
  • One line to reach us. Every product enquiry routes straight to our WhatsApp desk, no forms, no waiting on email threads.
